GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity goes a long way.

Who makes the fudge?

We are an independent reseller, trading as an extension of Find The Cash NZ. We do not manufacture, repackage or relabel fudge. Each listing must identify the actual manufacturer. Retail sourcing does not imply a partnership or endorsement by the retailer or brand.

Seller legal identity must be confirmed before opening. Contact for preview questions: findthecashnz@gmail.com.

Allergens, labels & safe storage

Ingredients, allergen declarations and precautionary warnings must be copied accurately from the specific sealed pack. Check the physical label every time; recipes can change. Never assume a pack is allergen-free. Contact the manufacturer if unsure. Keep the original packaging, batch and date marks intact and follow storage instructions.

Only verified, intact, shelf-stable packs belong in this shop. Recalled, damaged, expired or unsuitable packs must be withheld. Heat-safe delivery must be arranged before supply.

Pickup & shipping

Pickup locations, delivery coverage, charges and timeframes are not configured. No option is available until verified by the seller.

Do not send an address until the seller confirms that delivery is available. A pickup meeting point is shared privately after confirmation.

Returns, refunds & your rights

New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act rights apply to sales made in trade and cannot be excluded. If an item is unsafe, faulty, misdescribed or arrives damaged, contact the seller with order details and, where practical, photos and batch information. Do not consume suspected unsafe food. The appropriate remedy depends on the issue and may include replacement or refund. You do not have to contact the manufacturer instead of the seller.

Change-of-mind returns are not offered for food. This does not limit rights for faulty or unsafe goods. The seller must confirm any cancellation terms and delivery timing before accepting a request.

Privacy & contact

This app has no account, payment collection, analytics or advertising trackers. Your bag (product IDs and quantities only) is saved on this device until you clear it. Name and reply details remain in the current form until you clear or leave it. Preparing an email opens your mail app; nothing is sent automatically.

If you send a request, the seller and email provider receive the information you choose to send. It is used to answer your enquiry and fulfil a confirmed order. Necessary information may be shared with a delivery provider. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection logs. The seller must set a retention policy and privacy contact before launch. You can request access or correction through that contact.

Read the closed Android preview privacy policy

For questions about the current Android preview, contact findthecashnz@gmail.com. A business privacy-contact and retention policy will be confirmed before orders open.

Install the app

On Android, open the hosted shop in Chrome and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”. Installation needs HTTPS and browser support. The app shell can open offline; stock and order requests require a fresh online check. The Android project is also supplied separately for building and testing before a Google Play submission.
