# Before accepting a first sale in New Zealand

Prepared 23 October 2026. This is an operational checklist with official reference links, not a determination of your registration status or a legal compliance certificate.

1. **Confirm the business activity with MPI / your local council.** Selling only manufacturer-packaged shelf-stable food can fall within a registration exemption, but this depends on the actual food and activity. Do not assume all fudge is shelf-stable. Complete [MPI’s food-business registration guidance / My Food Rules](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-business/starting-a-food-business/register-food-business). MPI explains that even [exempt businesses must supply safe and suitable food](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-safety-home/how-to-buy-safe-food-online). Record the result and any registration/verification requirements privately. Do this before enabling requests.
2. **Source genuine sealed packs.** Buy from legitimate NZ retailers, keep itemised receipts, supplier/store details, purchase dates, batch/lot codes and quantities. Do not manufacture, split packs, rewrap or change labels under this plan. Avoid claims of partnership with supermarkets or manufacturers. Review supplier conditions before relying on ongoing supply.
3. **Inspect each batch.** Confirm intact packaging, appropriate remaining shelf life, actual storage requirements and no recall. Follow the label throughout storage and delivery, including protection from heat and contamination. Do not sell food past a use-by date. This shop conservatively withholds both past-use-by and past-best-before packs; the rules for those date marks are different.
4. **Provide accurate labels.** Copy ingredients, declared allergens, precautionary statements and full manufacturer information from the specific pack, and keep a legible label photograph privately. Include weight, batch, date mark, storage and applicable nutrition/origin and NZ/AU supplier-contact information. Do not invent dietary or allergen-free claims. [MPI food label guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-safety-home/how-read-food-labels) and [allergen guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-safety-home/food-allergies-intolerances) explain the risks and information to check.
5. **Have a recall process.** Maintain batch-to-purchase and batch-to-confirmed-order records privately so affected stock can be withheld and customers contacted. Check [MPI recall guidance](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/food-business/food-recalls/food-recall-guidance-for-businesses). Remove affected listings, quarantine stock and follow official instructions. The app cannot detect recalls for you.
6. **Identify the seller and make accurate offers.** You are buying to resell in trade. Supply your legal seller identity and working contact details. Show real prices in NZD including applicable taxes, delivery charges, coverage and realistic timeframes before agreement. Do not advertise stock you cannot reasonably supply or make unsupported savings/lowest-price claims. See [Commerce Commission: selling online](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/business/dealing-with-typical-situations/selling-goods-and-services/selling-online/).
7. **Respect consumer remedies.** Food is not exempt from quality/safety guarantees. A change-of-mind policy cannot remove statutory remedies for unsafe, faulty or misdescribed goods. Review the included wording and [Consumer Protection’s business obligations](https://www.consumerprotection.govt.nz/guidance-for-businesses/complying-with-consumer-laws/obligations-under-the-consumer-guarantees-act).
8. **Set a privacy policy that matches operations.** Choose a privacy officer/contact, what records are needed, access controls, a retention/deletion schedule and the actual email/carrier providers. Collect only necessary details; keep customer data out of public GitHub and web/config.json. Explain access/correction and any provider sharing. See [Privacy Commissioner: collecting information](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/collecting/) and [business obligations](https://www.privacy.org.nz/responsibilities/your-obligations/).
9. **Check costs before pricing.** Retail purchase price + packaging + delivery subsidy + losses + applicable tax/fees must be considered. Retail resale may not beat supermarket prices. No supplier, product or selling price has been chosen for you, and low costs/profit cannot be guaranteed.
10. **Verify the first complete journey.** Test real pack data, shipping and total, email destination, safe fulfilment, request reconfirmation and refunds/contact. Only then explicitly enable requests. No sale or payment is enabled by downloading this project.
