BEFORE WE OPEN

Good treats. Clear details.

We're not rushing into taking orders. There are a few important things to get right first.

01
⌕

Choose real packs

Source sealed, commercially made fudge. Confirm the original manufacturer and what is physically in stock.

02

03
♡

Confirm purchase costs, delivery coverage and genuine NZD prices before we start accepting requests.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered sweetly.

No guessing, no fake stock and no surprise checkout.

Can I buy fudge now? +

Not yet. This website and Android app are pre-launch previews only. There are no active products, prices, orders, checkout or payments. Follow our Facebook page for future updates.

Do you make the fudge yourself? +

No. We plan to be an independent reseller of genuine, commercially manufactured fudge from established stores. We will not repackage or relabel the products, and buying from a store does not imply an endorsement or partnership.

Will there be healthier options? +

We'd love to explore reduced-sugar or other options, but we'll only use nutrition and health descriptions that can be supported by the actual manufacturer's packaging. Nothing is confirmed yet.

What will it cost, and where can you ship? +

Prices, stock, delivery costs and supported locations have not been confirmed. We'll publish these only after checking real products, food resale requirements and delivery arrangements.

How do I contact you? +

Email findthecashnz@gmail.com or message our Facebook page. Please don't send payment details — we're not taking orders yet.
