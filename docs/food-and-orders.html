GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE WE OPEN

Sweet treats deserve
clear information.

We're preparing a proposed New Zealand fudge resale business. This page explains our plans and what we still need to verify before offering anything for sale.

SHOP NOT OPEN: No products, prices, orders, payment service, stock reservations, pickup or delivery service are currently offered. The page is information only and is not a consumer checkout or a sales contract.

Who makes the fudge?

We don't manufacture, cook, cut, repackage or relabel fudge ourselves. We plan to source commercially manufactured fudge and resell it in its original sealed retail packaging. A contract manufacturer might later produce packs bearing our brand, but no manufacturing agreement is confirmed. Retail sourcing does not mean we are endorsed by a retailer.

Ingredients, allergens and storage

Before a product is listed, we intend to check its exact manufacturer label, ingredients, declared allergens and warnings, date mark, net weight, supplier information and storage instructions. Fudge can contain milk, soy, nuts or other allergens depending on the recipe; we do not assume any variant is safe for particular diets. Check the actual sealed pack label each time; recipes may change.

We will only consider suitable, verified stock and will need appropriate temperature-safe storage, traceability and a recall process. MPI's official My Food Rules guidance ↗

Healthier or lower-sugar options

Reduced-sugar flavours are only an idea at this stage. We will not describe future fudge as “healthy”, “low sugar”, allergy-friendly or nutritionally beneficial unless the precise finished product meets applicable New Zealand/Australia food standards and claims can be substantiated. Food Standards claim guidance ↗

Prices, delivery and packaging

Actual product prices, GST treatment, pack weights, delivery regions, fees and dispatch timeframes have not been established. We will display the full price and applicable delivery charges before any future order is confirmed. Private home/pickup addresses will not be shown publicly.

Returns and refunds

There are no live orders yet. When sales begin, our process will honour applicable New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act rights, including remedies for unsafe, faulty or misdescribed food. A future change-of-mind policy cannot override legal guarantees. For safety concerns, don't consume a suspect product; retain its packaging and contact us with batch information. This is a pre-launch explanation, not a legal certification or evidence of food-business registration. Final policies will be updated when genuine products and fulfilment arrangements are confirmed.
