Privacy policy: Find The Cash NZ Fudge

Effective 23 October 2026 · Updated for planned synced preview v1.3 (4), 24 October 2026 · Android package nz.findthecash.fudge

This policy covers the previously distributed bundled Android preview (v1.1, and any v1.2 preview) and the planned synchronized read-only preview (v1.3). All are previews of a proposed New Zealand fudge resale brand, not operating stores. There are no listed products, active orders or payments in these builds.

Data used on your device

The app has no account or sign-in. In earlier bundled preview versions, a local bag stores only product IDs and quantities; the catalogue is empty. Any details typed into the disabled request form stay on the device and are not sent to us. Planned version 1.3 shows the information-only website directly and has no bag, checkout or user-entry forms. It bundles the same public pages for offline viewing.

Earlier preview versions provide “Clear bag & form details” for their local bag and form. The planned synchronized v1.3 has no bag or stored order details; tap Sync to reload public information when connected. Android’s app-storage controls or uninstalling the app clear local app data. The app disables Android backup for its data.

Collection, sharing and tracking

These preview versions do not submit names, contact details, purchases or payment information to a developer-operated server. They include no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDK. No location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission is requested. Planned v1.3 loads the same public pre-launch pages as the website over HTTPS from GitHub Pages and falls back to bundled pages when unavailable. Visiting the public pages may send normal network connection information (including IP address and standard request metadata) to GitHub, which operates the hosting service. No online shop or checkout is configured in this preview.

External links and contact

If you choose to open Facebook, this opens an external app or browser. Facebook and that app/browser process information under their own policies. If you voluntarily send an enquiry through an external service, that service and the recipient receive the information you choose to send. This is separate from the closed app’s local form.

For privacy questions or a request concerning information you have voluntarily sent, use the contact options on the Find The Cash NZ Facebook page. Do not send payment-card details or sensitive health information.

This privacy-policy website

This information-only page is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no forms, analytics, cookies or scripts added by us. GitHub may process ordinary connection information when you visit; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Before a shop launch

Any future version that enables requests, email checkout, delivery, payments, accounts or a hosted storefront needs a policy and Google Play Data safety declaration that accurately describe that version. This preview’s no-collection description must not be reused unchanged after those features are enabled.
