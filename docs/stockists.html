WHO WE ARE

We're building a brand.

Find The Cash NZ Fudge is a proposed retail confectionery project inspired by the Find The Cash NZ Facebook community. Our goal is accessible pricing and clear information for New Zealand shoppers.

We are not fudge manufacturers. We hope to work with an established food manufacturer that can make and seal compliant products for us.

What we're looking for.

  • Existing proven fudge recipes, rather than costly new recipe development
  • Small trial runs and a realistic minimum order quantity
  • Original sealed retail packaging with our branding, if available
  • Accurate label, nutrition, allergen, storage, shelf-life and batch details
  • Pricing, lead times and delivery options within New Zealand

OUR RETAIL GOAL

From one good product to one good local store.

We would love to start with local retail trials before approaching broader grocery ranging. This is an aspiration — no retail chain has accepted, endorsed or ranged our product, and no manufacturing agreement has been finalised.
